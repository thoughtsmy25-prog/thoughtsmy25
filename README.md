# Simple Interest Calculator

A beginner-friendly calculator for computing simple interest and the total amount.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Inputs

- **Principal (P):** Initial amount of money
- **Rate (R):** Annual interest rate in percent
- **Time (T):** Time period in years

## Example

For a principal of 1,000, an annual rate of 5%, and a period of 2 years:

- Simple Interest = (1,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
- Total Amount = 1,000 + 100 = 1,100

## Run

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser. Enter the principal, rate, and time, then select **Calculate**.
